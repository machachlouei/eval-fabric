## Context and problem statement

What is the situation that requires a decision? What is the question we are answering?

Two or three short paragraphs. Be specific about the constraints. The decision only makes sense in light of the context — without it, the next reader has no way to evaluate whether the decision still applies.

* Driver two (e.g., must be reproducible across runs)
* Driver three (e.g., must not introduce new infrastructure dependencies)
